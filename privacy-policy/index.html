Privacy Policy — LifeTycoon

Last updated: 31 August 2026

LifeTycoon ("the app", "we", "us") is a single-player life-simulation game for Android, developed by Aman Juneja ("the developer"). This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

Contact: amanjuneja75@gmail.com

LifeTycoon is currently in closed testing. This policy describes the data practices of the app including features that are being enabled during the testing period (advertising, analytics and optional cloud backup). Where a feature is not yet active in the build you have installed, the corresponding data is not collected.

Summary

  • The game is playable entirely offline and does not require an account.
  • Your character names and in-game choices stay on your device (and in your personal cloud backup only if you choose to enable it). They are never sold.
  • We use Google AdMob to show ads, and Google Firebase for crash reporting and anonymous usage analytics.
  • Google Sign-In is optional and is used only to back up your save file to your own private cloud storage.
  • The app is rated for teen audiences and is not directed to children under 13.

1. Data stored on your device

Your game progress — character details you enter, life events, statistics, settings and purchase status — is saved in the app's private storage on your device. This data does not leave your device unless you enable cloud backup (section 4). You can erase it at any time via Settings → Reset data, or by uninstalling the app.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the app shows ads supplied by Google AdMob. For this, Google may collect and process:

  • device advertising identifier, IP address, general device information (model, OS version, language, coarse region);
  • ad interaction data (impressions, clicks).

This is used to serve and measure ads and to limit repetition. Where required (e.g. the EEA, UK), the app shows Google's consent prompt before any personalised advertising. See Google's Advertising Privacy & Terms.

You can opt out of ads entirely by purchasing the one-time LifeTycoon Pro unlock.

3. Analytics and crash reporting (Google Firebase)

The app uses Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics to understand stability and general usage. Data processed includes:

  • a randomly generated app-instance identifier;
  • device model, OS version, app version, language, coarse region;
  • crash stack traces and diagnostic logs;
  • aggregate in-app events (e.g. screen opened, life started) — no character names or free-text you enter.

This data is not used to identify you personally and is not sold. See the Firebase Privacy documentation.

4. Optional account and cloud backup (Google Sign-In)

If you choose Settings → Back up to cloud, you can sign in with your Google account. In that case we store:

  • your Google account ID and email address (via Firebase Authentication);
  • a copy of your game save file, in a private document that only your account can read or write (Google Cloud Firestore).

Nothing else is uploaded — no contacts, no location, no device address book. This feature is entirely optional; the game is fully playable without it.

Deleting your account and cloud data: use Settings → Delete cloud backup & account in the app, or email amanjuneja75@gmail.com with the account address. This permanently removes your authentication record and your stored save file. Data on your device is separate and is removed with Reset data or by uninstalling.

5. Purchases

The one-time "LifeTycoon Pro" purchase is processed by Google Play Billing. We receive a purchase token confirming the entitlement; we do not receive or store your name, card number or billing address. Refunds and payment records are handled by Google Play.

6. Data we do not collect

The app does not collect precise location, contacts, photos, files, microphone audio, SMS, call logs, or health data.

7. Children

LifeTycoon is rated for teen audiences (IARC Teen / PEGI 12 / ESRB Teen). It is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Data sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data is shared only with the service providers named above (Google: AdMob, Firebase, Play Billing), acting as our processors or — for advertising — as independent controllers under their own policies. We may disclose data if required by law.

9. Data retention

  • On-device data: until you reset or uninstall.
  • Cloud backup: until you delete it (section 4) or the account is inactive and removed.
  • Analytics/crash data: retained per Google Firebase's standard retention windows.

10. Your rights

Depending on your location you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data. For anything held by us (the optional account and cloud save), email amanjuneja75@gmail.com. For data held by Google, use your Google Account controls.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, noted in the app.
