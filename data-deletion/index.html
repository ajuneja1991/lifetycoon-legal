Delete your data — LifeTycoon

Last updated: 28 September 2026

This page explains how to delete data connected with LifeTycoon (com.ajuneja.life_tycoon), the Android game by Aman Juneja. LifeTycoon has no accounts, so there is no account to close. Your game lives on your device; the only data held elsewhere is anonymous analytics and crash data kept by Google Firebase, and advertising data kept by Google.

Option 1 — Delete from inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open LifeTycoon.
  2. Go to Settings → Delete my data.
  3. Confirm.

This immediately:

  • deletes your current life, dynasty, saves and preferences on this device;
  • requests deletion of the Firebase Analytics data associated with this install, and turns analytics collection off.

Kept: your unlocked achievements and a genuine LifeTycoon Pro purchase, so you are not charged again. To remove those too, uninstall the app (see Option 2).

Option 2 — Uninstall the app

Uninstalling LifeTycoon removes everything the app stored on your device, including achievements. Your Pro purchase stays on your Google Play account and can be restored if you reinstall — manage or refund it in the Google Play Store under Payments & subscriptions.

Option 3 — Ask us by email

If you can no longer open the app, or you want to ask about anything else, email lifetycoon.support@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data — LifeTycoon". Tell us:

  • that you want your LifeTycoon data deleted; and
  • if you can, the approximate date you installed the app and your device model, so we can find the related crash reports.

Because the app has no accounts and never sends your name or email to us, we cannot link analytics data to you personally. We will delete any crash reports and correspondence we can identify from your details, and delete your email thread once the request is closed.

We acknowledge requests within 7 days and complete them within 30 days.

What is deleted, and what is kept

Data Where it lives What happens
Game progress, lives, dynasty, settings Your device only Deleted by Option 1 or 2
Achievements Your device only Kept by Option 1; deleted by Option 2
Anonymous usage analytics Google Firebase Deletion requested by Option 1; otherwise removed automatically when Firebase's retention period ends
Crash reports Google Firebase Crashlytics Removed automatically when Crashlytics' retention period ends (about 90 days); on request under Option 3 where we can identify them
Advertising data Google (AdMob) Held by Google under its own policies — reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads
Purchase records Google Play Kept by Google as required for payments and tax records
Support emails Our support inbox Deleted once your request is closed
